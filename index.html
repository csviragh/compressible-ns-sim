Self-propulsion (active fluid)

Pairwise repulsion (retarded)

f(x,t) = A Σy ρ(y, t − |x−y|/vint) K(|x−y|) êy→x h²

2D Coulomb: f = −A∇φ with −∇²φ = 2π(ρ − ρ̄) on the periodic unit square, i.e. f(x,t) = A Σy≠x ρ(y, t − dmin/vint) (x−y)/|x−y|² h² summed over all periodic images (Ewald-summed kernel, uniform background subtracted). vint = ∞: FFT, O(N log N). Retarded: direct sum over all N−1 offsets, O(N²) per step (delay = minimum-image distance / vint, rounded to Δt) — use WebGPU or a coarse grid (≤ 64² on CPU).

K(d) = [(R0/d)α − 1](1 − d/R0)² for 0 < d < R0 (own cell excluded): divergent at d → 0, K(R0) = K′(R0) = 0. α < 2 keeps the 2D continuum force integral finite; α = 0 would give K ≡ 0, so α ≥ 0.1. Only the repulsion is retarded (Eulerian ring buffer of past ρ); the Lamé term and the fluid stay instantaneous and compressible.

Order parameter / correlations

Over pairs of cells i ≠ j with direction v̂ = u/|u| (cells slower than 2 % of the mean speed have no direction and are left out): the distribution of cos θij = v̂i·v̂j (30 000 random pairs) and its exact mean ⟨cos θij⟩ = (|Σv̂|² − N)/(N(N − 1)) ≈ φdir² — 1 for a perfect flock, 0 for random directions. C(r) = ⟨v̂(x)·v̂(x + r)⟩ (blue) and the connected C(r) of the fluctuations δv̂ = v̂ − ⟨v̂⟩ (green, Cavagna et al. 2010), radially averaged via FFT (wrapping on periodic axes, zero-padded at walls). ξ = first zero of the connected C(r).

About the model
∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u = μ∇²u + (λ+μ)∇(∇·u) − (1/τ)(|u| − v₀)u/|u| + f

Like the Stable Fluids solver of navier-stokes-sim, but with no pressure and no projection, so the velocity is free to compress. Shear (transverse) modes damp at μk², compressive (longitudinal) modes at (λ+2μ)k²; stability needs μ ≥ 0 and λ + 2μ ≥ 0 (λ is clamped). A passive density obeys ∂ρ/∂t + ∇·(ρu) = 0 (finite volume, mass conserved) with no feedback on u. The viscous step is exact in Fourier space; advection is semi-Lagrangian.
