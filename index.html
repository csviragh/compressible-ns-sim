About the model
∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u = μ∇²u + (λ+μ)∇(∇·u) − (1/τ)(|u| − v₀)u/|u| + f

Like the Stable Fluids solver of navier-stokes-sim, but with no pressure and no projection, so the velocity is free to compress. Shear (transverse) modes damp at μk², compressive (longitudinal) modes at (λ+2μ)k²; stability needs μ ≥ 0 and λ + 2μ ≥ 0 (λ is clamped). The viscous step is exact in Fourier space; advection is semi-Lagrangian.
